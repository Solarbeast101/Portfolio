My story

More than a farmer.
A steward of tomorrow.

Farming has taught me that the best things are built slowly. As a cotton farmer, I show up for the soil, the plants, and the people who depend on the harvest — through sunny mornings, difficult seasons, and everything in between.

As a BSIT student at University of Sacnarlos, I also believe tradition and technology belong together. I’m learning how thoughtful digital tools can help farmers work smarter, share knowledge, and grow with confidence.
